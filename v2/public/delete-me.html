Privacy Policy

Delete your account

You can delete your StreetSweep account and its data at any time.

Deleted

  • Your account, email address and profile picture
  • All your drives and location history
  • Your places and their photos
  • Your personal vehicles, phones and loggers
  • Your team memberships and achievements

What happens to teams

  • Streets only you drove come off your teams' maps
  • Teams you own alone pass to their longest-serving admin or member
  • Teams with nobody else in them are closed
  • Streets you marked done for a team stay marked, without your name

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. See the privacy policy for details.
