1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, display name and password. We store passwords only as a salted hash and never in plain text.
  • Team and vehicle information: teams you create or join, vehicle names and details, and which vehicles you drive.
  • Content you add: places, notes, areas you draw, streets you mark as done or left out, and photos you choose to attach to places or vehicles.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.

Information collected when you use the Service

  • Precise location: GPS position, speed, heading and time while a drive is being recorded, from your phone or from a StreetSweep logger. See Location and background location.
  • Drive records: the routes you drive, the streets they cover, distance, duration, drive type and the vehicle used.
  • Physical activity: if you allow it, the app uses your phone's activity recognition to detect when you start or stop driving. This signal is used on your device and is not stored as a separate record.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, and identifiers for phones and loggers linked to your account. When you pair a logger, the app connects to it over Bluetooth to set it up.
  • Log information: IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps, recorded by our servers for security and troubleshooting.
  • Cookies: the website uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: record drives, match them to streets, show your coverage and progress, and share coverage with your teams.
  • To create and secure your account, including sending sign-up and password reset codes by email.
  • To calculate statistics, achievements and team leaderboards.
  • To keep the Service running, fix problems, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. How we share information

  • With your teams: when you belong to a team, its members can see the drives, coverage, vehicles and places you share with that team, attributed to you by display name. Team administrators can see team membership and assign vehicles.
  • Service providers: companies that host our servers and deliver our email, acting on our behalf and only as needed to provide those services.
  • Map services: to show maps and search addresses, our servers request map tiles and search results from OpenStreetMap-based services. Those requests come from our servers, not your device, and do not include your account details. Address searches you type are sent as search text.
  • Legal reasons: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, subpoena or other legal process, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of StreetSweep, our users or others.
  • Business transfers: if StreetSweep is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you ask us to share it.

4. Location and background location

StreetSweep's core feature is recording the streets you drive, so it needs your precise location.

  • The app collects location only while a drive is being recorded. A notification is shown for as long as recording is active.
  • If you grant "Allow all the time" location access, the app can keep recording when the screen is off or another app (such as navigation) is in front, and can start and stop drives automatically. Location is still only collected during a recorded drive.
  • Recorded drives are uploaded to our servers so your coverage is kept with your account and shared with the teams you choose.
  • You can stop recording at any time, turn off automatic recording, or revoke location permission in your device settings. Without location access the app cannot record drives.

5. Data retention

We keep your account information, drives and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep it to comply with law, resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. Streets you marked as done for a team may stay marked, without your name. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your StreetSweep account and its data at any time at streetsweep.net/delete-me, or from your account page:

  • Signed in: confirm with your password and your account is deleted immediately.
  • Can't sign in: enter your email address, confirm it with the code we send you, and an administrator will delete your account within 30 days. We email you when it's done.

You can also delete individual drives and places in the app or on the website, or email privacy@streetsweep.net.

Deleted: your account profile and email address, drives and location history, places and photos, personal vehicles, phones and loggers linked to your account, team memberships and achievements.

Kept: shared teams carry on without you. A team you own alone passes to its longest-serving admin or member. Streets you marked as done or left out for a team stay marked, without your name. We keep a record that your deletion request was carried out (your email address, name and the date), and records we must retain by law. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your account details on your account page, and ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Permissions: you can change location, activity, Bluetooth, notification and photo permissions in your device settings at any time.
  • Teams: you can leave a team at any time. Your future drives will no longer be shared with it.
  • Email: we send only account and security email (such as sign-up and reset codes). We do not send marketing email.

8. US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, or its use for targeted advertising or profiling (we do none of these);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information, such as precise geolocation (we use it only to provide the Service you request); and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email privacy@streetsweep.net. We will verify your request by confirming it from the email address on your account. You may use an authorized agent, who must provide proof of authorization. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

9. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, per-device access tokens and access controls on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for drivers and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where state law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where we store data

StreetSweep is operated from the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the US, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in the US.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use your personal information, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
