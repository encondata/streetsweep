    Tools

    Click the map to drop corners. Drag a corner to move it, click the small dot between two corners to add one there, and right-click a corner to remove it.

    Clicking near an area you have already drawn puts the corner exactly on its edge. Two corners along the same edge bring the whole stretch between them, so neighbouring areas share one line rather than two that nearly meet.
